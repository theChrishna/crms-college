Campus Infrastructure & Facilities

Providing an Environment Conducive to Academic Focus, Practical Training & Sports

Learning Environment

Academic & Campus Amenities

Our Bharnakalan campus integrates academic, practical, and sports infrastructure to support well-rounded student growth.

Sports Grounds & Physical Fitness

Spacious open green grounds suitable for outdoor games including cricket, football, volleyball, kabaddi, and athletics. Annual sports meets encourage healthy physical competition and teamwork.

Spacious Lecture Classrooms

Well-ventilated, naturally illuminated classrooms with comfortable seating furniture, blackboard/whiteboard equipment, and acoustics tailored for focused teacher-student interaction.

Administrative & Student Service Wing

Houses the Principal's chamber, staff room, inquiry counter, scholarship cell, and fee counters for hassle-free student services and prompt assistance.

College Library & Reading Room

Maintains a rich repository of curriculum reference books, textbooks for B.A., B.Sc., B.Com., competitive examination guides, regional newspapers, and scholarly periodicals.

Science Laboratories

Dedicated laboratories for Physics, Chemistry, Zoology, and Botany equipped with standard experimental apparatus, glassware, chemicals, and safety guidelines for university practical coursework.

Drinking Water, Power & Safety

Equipped with Reverse Osmosis (RO) purified drinking water coolers, generator-backed power supply, clean sanitation facilities for boys and girls, and round-the-clock security surveillance.
