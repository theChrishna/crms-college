Exam 📅 October 10, 2026 NEW

Dr. B. R. Ambedkar University Semester Examination Form Notification

All enrolled students are notified that online examination forms for upcoming odd-semester university examinations will be filled as per DBRAU Agra schedule. Ensure web registration credentials are valid before fee submission.

Annual National Sports & Physical Fitness Week

The college will celebrate Annual Sports Week featuring inter-departmental track, cricket, volleyball, and athletic competitions on campus grounds. Interested participants must register their names with the physical education incharge.

General 📅 September 20, 2026

Mandatory Anti-Ragging Undertaking for All Enrolled Students

In strict compliance with UGC and Hon'ble Supreme Court regulations, all students must submit their online anti-ragging affidavits through the national portal (antiragging.in) and deposit hard copy acknowledgments with the college office.

Scholarship Form Verification & Submission Circular (UP Govt Portal)
