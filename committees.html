Zero Tolerance

Anti-Ragging Committee & Squad

Constituted under UGC Regulations on Curbing the Menace of Ragging in Higher Educational Institutions.

🚨 Zero Tolerance Policy: Ragging in any form is strictly banned inside and outside the college campus.
National 24x7 Anti-Ragging Toll-Free Helpline: 1800-180-5522 | Email: helpline@antiragging.in

Mandate & Purpose: In compliance with the directives of the Hon'ble Supreme Court of India and UGC notifications, the Anti-Ragging Committee monitors, prevents, and eliminates any conduct by words spoken or written, or by an act which has the effect of teasing, treating, or handling with rudeness a fresher or any other student.

How to File an RTI Application

1. Submit a formal application in Hindi or English addressed to the Public Information Officer (PIO).
2. Enclose the prescribed statutory fee (Postal Order / Demand Draft / Cash receipt) as per UP State RTI Rules.
3. Information will be provided within the statutory period of 30 days from the date of receipt.

Campus Discipline

Rules, Regulations & Code of Conduct

General Guidelines for Students, Faculty and Campus Visitors.

For Students

  • Possession and display of valid College Identity Card is mandatory at all times on campus.
  • A minimum of 75% attendance in lectures and practical sessions is strictly required for semester examination eligibility.
  • Use of mobile phones inside classrooms, laboratories, and the library is prohibited.
  • Tobacco, smoking, intoxicants, and loud disruptive behavior are strictly banned and punishable with disciplinary expulsion.

For Teachers & Staff

  • Punctuality, professional ethics, and dedicated execution of university academic duties.
  • Fair, impartial, and mentoring attitude towards every student without bias.
  • Strict non-engagement in private commercial tuition or unauthorized outside employment.
  • Active participation in examination duties, NAAC/accreditation records, and co-curricular programs.
