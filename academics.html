Undergraduate Programmes (NEP 2020)

Degree curricula designed to foster foundational knowledge, analytical thinking, and vocational preparedness.

Faculty of Arts

Bachelor of Arts (B.A.)

Apply for B.A. →

The Bachelor of Arts is a three-year undergraduate programme providing a balanced education in humanities, social sciences, and languages. The curriculum follows the Choice-Based Credit System (CBCS) under National Education Policy 2020 as adopted by Dr. B. R. Ambedkar University, Agra.

Programme Duration 3 Years (6 Semesters) under NEP 2020 Framework
Sanctioned Intake [PLACEHOLDER: Official Sanctioned Seats, e.g. 400]
Minimum Eligibility Passed 10+2 (Intermediate) examination in any stream (Arts, Science, or Commerce) from UP Board, CBSE, ICSE, or any recognized State/National Board.
Subject Combinations Offered Students may select approved subject combinations from:
  • Hindi Literature
  • English Literature
  • History
  • Political Science
  • Sociology
  • Economics

Faculty of Science

Bachelor of Science (B.Sc.)

Apply for B.Sc. →

The Bachelor of Science programme emphasizes foundational science concepts, analytical skills, and hands-on laboratory experiments. Students are trained to understand natural laws, quantitative problem-solving, and empirical scientific reasoning.

Programme Duration 3 Years (6 Semesters) under NEP 2020 Framework
Sanctioned Intake [PLACEHOLDER: Official Sanctioned Seats, e.g. 120]
Minimum Eligibility Passed 10+2 (Intermediate) with Science stream (PCM / ZBC) with minimum qualifying percentage from recognized board.
Subject Groups Offered 1. Physical Sciences (PCM Group): Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics
2. Life Sciences (ZBC Group): Zoology, Botany, Chemistry
Laboratory Practical Work Mandatory weekly laboratory sessions in Physics, Chemistry, and Biological sciences.
Fee Structure [PLACEHOLDER: State Govt / University Prescribed Fee] (View Fee Details)
Faculty of Commerce

Bachelor of Commerce (B.Com.)

Apply for B.Com. →

The Bachelor of Commerce degree trains students in corporate financial reporting, banking operations, taxation legislation, commercial management, and business jurisprudence.

Programme Duration 3 Years (6 Semesters) under NEP 2020 Framework
Sanctioned Intake [PLACEHOLDER: Official Sanctioned Seats, e.g. 120]
Minimum Eligibility Passed 10+2 (Intermediate) with Commerce or allied stream from recognized examination board.
Core Subjects Financial Accounting, Business Regulatory Framework, Business Statistics, Corporate Law, Auditing, Income Tax Law and Accounts.
