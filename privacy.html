Institutional Data Protection, Student Privacy & Information Disclosure Guidelines

Institutional Policy

Data Protection & Privacy Standards

Effective Date: September 01, 2026 · Institution: Captain Rakesh Mohan Shyam Degree College, Bharnakalan, Mathura (Code: 0538)

Captain Rakesh Mohan Shyam Degree College ("the College", "we", "our") is dedicated to upholding the highest standards of confidentiality, transparency, and personal data protection for applicants, enrolled students, alumni, guardians, faculty members, and website visitors.

1. Information We Collect

When you browse this website, submit an online admission enquiry, or register for institutional notifications, we may collect the following limited categories of personal information:

  • Applicant Identity: Full Name, Father's/Mother's/Guardian's Name, Date of Birth, Gender, and Category (for statutory reservation compliance).
  • Contact Details: Valid Mobile Phone Number, Residential Address, and Email Address.
  • Academic Profile: Prior qualifying examination details (Intermediate / 10+2 roll number, marks, subjects), and choice of degree programme (B.A., B.Sc., or B.Com.).
  • Technical Access Logs: Standard non-personally identifiable server access logs (browser client type, timestamp, requested URI) for network security and operational diagnostics.

2. Purpose of Collection & Use

Personal data gathered through the college website or administrative forms is strictly utilized for legitimate institutional, academic, and regulatory functions:

  • Responding to course queries, prospectus requests, and pre-admission counseling inquiries.
  • Processing enrollment, subject allocation, and university examination registrations with Dr. B. R. Ambedkar University, Agra.
  • Verification and submission for Uttar Pradesh state post-matric scholarship schemes and fee reimbursement portals.
  • Dispatch of emergency college advisories, examination schedules, fee reminders, and statutory notices.

3. Data Protection, Sharing & Disclosure

We maintain strict safeguards against unauthorized access, alterations, or leaks. We do not sell, rent, commercialize, or share student contact details or data with any private third-party marketing agencies.

Information disclosure occurs solely under legitimate statutory requirements:

  • Affiliating University: Submission of statutory student records to Dr. B. R. Ambedkar University, Agra for official enrollment numbers and degree issuance.
  • Government Bodies: Department of Higher Education (UP), Social Welfare Department, and regulatory authorities as required by prevailing law.
  • Law Enforcement & Judiciary: Where required by valid judicial order, summons, or statutory compliance.

4. Retention & Archival Policies

General prospective applicant enquiries are maintained for the duration of the corresponding academic intake cycle (up to 12 months) and then securely archived or purged. Academic records of enrolled and graduated students are preserved indefinitely in accordance with university registry mandates and government higher education archive protocols.

5. Student Rights & Data Rectification

Students and prospective candidates have the right to inspect, verify, and request correction of any inaccurate clerical records or outdated contact information held in the college administrative database by presenting a written request with photo identification to the college office.
