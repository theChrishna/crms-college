Privacy Policy

How Captain Rakesh Mohan Shyam Degree College Handles Information & Data Protection

Effective Date: September 01, 2026
Captain Rakesh Mohan Shyam Degree College ("the College", "we", "our") is committed to protecting the privacy of prospective applicants, enrolled students, parents, faculty, and website visitors.

1. Information We Collect

When you interact with our website or submit an online enquiry/admission form, we collect only necessary identifying information, including:

  • Full Name
  • Contact details (Mobile phone number and optional email address)
  • Course of interest and educational background
  • Technical logs (standard non-personally identifiable server access logs, browser types)

2. Purpose of Collection & Use

Collected information is used strictly for institutional purposes:

  • Responding to admission inquiries and counseling guidance
  • Processing official admission applications and verifying examination eligibility with Dr. B. R. Ambedkar University, Agra
  • Government scholarship verification and mandatory regulatory reporting
  • Administrative notices and emergency college communication

3. Data Protection & Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade student or visitor personal data to any private commercial marketing entity. Information is shared only with:

  • Dr. B. R. Ambedkar University, Agra for enrollment and examination records
  • Government departments (e.g., Social Welfare Department for scholarships) as required by law
  • Law enforcement authorities where mandated by judicial summons or statutory requirements

4. Retention Period

Enquiry data is retained for the active academic admission cycle (typically up to one academic year) and securely archived or purged thereafter. Academic records of admitted students are preserved as statutory university student registries.

5. Contacting the College
