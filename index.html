About The College

Rooted in Values, Committed to Academic Growth

Captain Rakesh Mohan Shyam Degree College is established with the vision of providing accessible, high-quality higher education to students of Bharnakalan, Mathura, and adjoining rural regions.

Affiliated to Dr. B. R. Ambedkar University, Agra, the institution adheres strictly to university syllabi, state educational policies, and the National Education Policy (NEP) 2020. Our sprawling green campus offers an intellectually invigorating environment, spacious lecture halls, practical labs, and comprehensive student support.
