Higher Education Grounded in Academic Rigor, Character & Purpose.

Located in Bharnakalan, Mathura, Captain Rakesh Mohan Shyam Degree College delivers accessible, high-caliber undergraduate degree education under the statutory curriculum of Dr. B. R. Ambedkar University, Agra. We empower regional scholars in Arts, Science, and Commerce with intellectual discipline, laboratory training, and ethical leadership.

Admissions Pathway

Your 4-Step Journey to a University Degree

Transparent, straightforward enrollment guided by Dr. B. R. Ambedkar University merit criteria.

01

Explore Courses

Review eligibility criteria for B.A., B.Sc., or B.Com. and select your subject combinations in consultation with our faculty.

02

Submit Application

Obtain the admission form from the college office or submit an online enquiry with 10+2 marksheet and identification documents.

03

Merit & Verification

Original academic credential verification, DBRAU university registration, and category/scholarship eligibility validation.

04

Enrollment & ID

Fee deposit with official receipt, issue of student identity card, orientation, and regular semester class commencement.
