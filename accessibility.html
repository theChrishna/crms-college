Accessibility Statement

Commitment to Digital Inclusivity & WCAG 2.1 AA Compliance Standards

Captain Rakesh Mohan Shyam Degree College is committed to ensuring that our website is accessible to people of all abilities, including individuals with visual, auditory, cognitive, or motor impairments.

1. Compliance Standards

We aim to conform to Level AA of the World Wide Web Consortium (W3C) Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1. These guidelines explain how to make web content more accessible for people with disabilities and user-friendly for everyone.

2. Implemented Accessibility Features

  • Keyboard Accessibility: All interactive elements, menus, dropdowns, forms, and dialogs can be operated via standard keyboard navigation (Tab, Enter, Escape, Arrow keys).
  • Skip to Main Content: A dedicated skip navigation link is provided at the very top of each page for screen reader and keyboard users.
  • Color Contrast: Text colors meet or exceed the mandatory 4.5:1 contrast ratio against their respective background surfaces.
  • Semantic HTML5 Structure: Proper heading hierarchy (h1, h2, h3), landmarks (header, nav, main, footer, aside), and table structures with table headers.
  • Text Scaling & Pinch-to-Zoom: Viewport settings do not disable or restrict zooming. Content adapts gracefully up to 200% zoom without horizontal scrolling breaks.
  • Alternative Text: Informative alternative text (alt attributes) is provided for campus photographs, emblems, and diagrams.
  • Mobile-First Design: Touch targets are sized at a minimum of 44x44 pixels to ensure comfortable tapping on small touch screens.
