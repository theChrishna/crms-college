About Our Institution

Identity, Governance, Vision, and University Affiliation

Institutional Profile

Vision, Mission & Core Values

Established to nurture intellectual curiosity, moral grounding, and accessible degree education.

🎯

Our Vision

To be a premier institution of undergraduate learning in the Mathura region, recognized for academic discipline, student-centric pedagogy, and fostering young leaders equipped with knowledge, ethical convictions, and societal commitment.

🌱

Our Mission

To impart affordable, quality higher education adhering to National Education Policy (NEP 2020) guidelines; to promote scientific inquiry and humanistic values; and to ensure equitable educational opportunities for boys and girls from rural backgrounds.

Our Core Values

Academic Integrity

Truth, fairness, and scholarly honesty in all academic endeavors.

Inclusivity

Welcoming all students irrespective of economic, social, or geographic background.

Civic Responsibility

Instilling patriotic spirit, discipline, and community service values.

Continuous Improvement

Constant upgrading of teaching practices and campus facilities.
