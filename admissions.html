Admissions (Session 2026–27)

Eligibility Guidelines, Admission Procedure, Fee Structure & Refund Policy

Admission Guidelines

Step-by-Step Admission Procedure

Admissions to undergraduate courses (B.A., B.Sc., B.Com.) are conducted in accordance with rules framed by Dr. B. R. Ambedkar University, Agra.

01

Prospectus & Form

Obtain the official college admission application form and prospectus from the administrative counter at the campus in Bharnakalan, or fill the online enquiry form.

02

Document Verification

Submit completed application along with self-attested copies of 10th and 12th marksheets, certificates, Aadhaar card, and category certificate (if applicable).

03

Merit & Counseling

Admission is granted strictly based on academic merit in the qualifying 10+2 examination and university guidelines. Attend the counseling session on scheduled dates.

04

Fee Deposit & Enrolment

Pay the prescribed fee at the college fee counter, obtain official receipt, complete university Web Registration, and secure your admission ID card.

Mandatory Documents Required at Admission

  • High School (10th) Marksheet & Certificate (Original + 3 Copies)
  • Intermediate (12th) Marksheet & Certificate (Original + 3 Copies)
  • Transfer Certificate (T.C.) & Character Certificate (Original)
  • Migration Certificate (for candidates from boards other than UP Board)
  • Aadhaar Card copy of student
  • Recent passport size color photographs (5 copies)
  • Caste Certificate (for SC / ST / OBC candidates, as per UP Govt norms)
  • Income Certificate (for fee concession / scholarship applicants)
  • Anti-Ragging Undertaking (signed by student and parent)

Fee Refund Policy

In compliance with the University Grants Commission (UGC) guidelines and Dr. B. R. Ambedkar University, Agra directives regarding refund of fees:

  • Withdrawal before the formally notified last date of admission: 100% of the aggregate fee refunded after deducting a processing charge not exceeding ₹1,000/-.
  • Withdrawal within 15 days after the formally notified last date: Refund granted in accordance with UGC percentage slab norms upon written application.
  • Caution / Security Money: 100% refundable after course completion or upon leaving the college with valid clearance.
  • Application Procedure: Student must submit an official written cancellation request signed by the student and guardian to the Principal's Office along with original fee receipts.
